# Techno International New Town website roadmap

1. **Public website surface — completed.** The branded homepage, programme catalogue, individual course views, admissions guide, contact page and campus directions are responsive and fully connected. Course and contact content is maintained in a single fixture module for straightforward review.
2. **Institutional content expansion.** Add verified pages for the institute history, leadership, departments, faculty, facilities, research, committees, mandatory disclosures and accreditation. Replace provisional programme metadata with the current approved prospectus.
3. **News and campus life.** Add structured announcements, events, notices, galleries, placement stories and student activities, with an editorial workflow for institute staff.
4. **Admissions operations.** Connect the enquiry experience to Netlify Forms, publish downloadable fee and eligibility documents, and add programme-specific counselling information after owner approval.
5. **Content governance and launch review.** Complete accessibility and content-owner review, confirm every phone number and policy link, add analytics and search metadata, and establish an image/licensing register for official campus photography.
